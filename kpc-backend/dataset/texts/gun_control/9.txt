					Who We Are

				

			

          

        

      
		

		

	







    


    
      
	
	
		
    		
			
				



















    
                              We Empower Women to Effectively Protect Themselves, and Their Families:
	By Reaffirming that the Second Amendment right to bear arms "shall not be infringed," and that any attempt to do so is unacceptable.
	By Educating all interested individuals on the importance of properly and safely handling firearms.
	By Keeping voters informed about legislative efforts on the state and federal level regarding the use, sale, transfer, regulation, and ownership of firearms.
	By Providing a counter-argument to the gun control movement utilizing data and research to refute emotion and political agendas.
